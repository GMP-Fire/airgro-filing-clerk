Filing Clerk

A personal automation that files bank statements and other documents of record from Gmail into Google Drive.

Filing Clerk is a single-user script. It runs on a nightly schedule, looks for attachments from a known list of senders — banks, insurers, the tax authority — and saves them into the correct folder in the owner's own Google Drive.

It is not a product, has no users other than its author, and is not offered as a service.

Access it uses

  • Gmail, read-only. To find matching messages and download their attachments.
  • Google Drive. To read its own rules file and write filed documents into existing folders.
